Bridgeport Digital Tach firmware

Latest version: v9.5B

Update over Wi-Fi

For installed gauges. You need a phone. Nothing has to be unplugged. The spindle must be stopped.

  1. First, while your phone still has internet, download the update file:

    Download update file
  2. On the gauge, tap the gear in the middle of the dial (or swipe down), then tap Wi-Fi download. The gauge shows a QR code, with the network name and password under it.
  3. Scan the QR code with your phone's camera and join the network Bridgeport-RPM. If the phone warns there's no internet, choose to stay connected.
    No camera handy? Open Wi-Fi settings, pick Bridgeport-RPM and type the password shown on the gauge. It is a new password every time.
  4. The gauge's page may open by itself. If not, come back to this page and tap:

    Open the gauge's page
    Or type the full address into Chrome, including the http:// at the start:
    http://192.168.4.1
    Without the http:// some browsers search for it or try a secure address the gauge doesn't have, and say they can't reach this page.
  5. At the bottom of the gauge's page, tap Choose file, pick the file you downloaded, then tap Update firmware. Keep the page open until it says Update installed (about a minute). The gauge shows the progress too.
  6. The gauge restarts by itself on the new version. Switch your phone back to your normal Wi-Fi. Done.
Wi-Fi switches itself off after 10 minutes, or as soon as the spindle starts. STOP Wi-Fi on the gauge switches it off straight away. Your settings, tool presets and run-time totals are not affected. If the update doesn't finish, nothing is changed and the gauge carries on with the version it had.

To check the version: on the gauge, swipe up twice. The SETUP page shows it at the top.

Joined the Wi-Fi but the gauge's page won't open?
  • Use Chrome. Other browsers (Edge, Samsung Internet) may refuse addresses that don't start with https://.
  • If a small sign-in window popped up, close it and open the address in your normal browser instead. The pop-up window can't pick files.
  • Type the address with the http:// at the start, exactly as shown above.
  • Turn off mobile data while you update, so the phone doesn't go around the gauge's Wi-Fi.
  • Check the phone is still joined to Bridgeport-RPM. Some phones switch back to another Wi-Fi when there's no internet.
